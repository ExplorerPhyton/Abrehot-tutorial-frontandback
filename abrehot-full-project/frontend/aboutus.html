Abrehot Online Tutorials

About Us

Abrehot Online Tutorials was inspired by a simple idea from Mr. Abraham, a high school Chemistry teacher at Diamond Academy, CMC Branch in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Through his experience in the classroom, Mr. Abraham recognized that many students need extra academic support outside of school. At the same time, he saw that many qualified teachers have the knowledge, skills, and willingness to help students beyond the classroom but often lack an easy way to connect with those who need them. With this in mind, he envisioned a platform that would make tutoring more accessible for everyone. Rather than focusing on a single subject, he wanted to create a place where parents and students could easily find qualified tutors for all subjects, from Kindergarten (KG) to Grade 12. He also wanted tutors to have a trusted platform where they could share their expertise, set their own schedules and prices, and reach more students. To ensure the platform can be used by as many families as possible, Abrehot Online Tutorials is designed to support English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo, and Tigrinya. Parents and students can search for tutors, compare their qualifications and rates, and book online or in-person tutoring sessions with confidence. Our goal is simple: to make quality tutoring more affordable, easier to access, and available to every family while creating meaningful teaching opportunities for qualified educators. We believe that every student deserves the chance to succeed with the right guidance, and every dedicated teacher deserves the opportunity to make a difference beyond the classroom. Abrehot Online Tutorials is built on that vision—bringing students, parents, and tutors together to support learning, build confidence, and help every student reach their full potential.

Our Mission

Our mission at Abrehot Online Tutorials is to connect students and parents with qualified tutors who provide accessible, affordable, and personalized learning support. We aim to make quality education easier to access by creating a trusted platform where students can find the right guidance for their academic needs and where educators can share their knowledge and skills with more learners.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a leading tutoring platform in Ethiopia that helps every student access quality education regardless of location, language, or subject. We aspire to create a learning community where students achieve their academic goals, parents find reliable educational support, and tutors have meaningful opportunities to make a positive impact.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

  1. Who can use Abrehot Online Tutorials?

    2. Parents and students from Kindergarten (KG) to Grade 12 can use our platform to find tutoring support. Tutors can apply to become verified tutors on this platform where they can support students and make money on the side.

  2. What subjects are available?

    3. Students can find tutors for various subjects including Mathematics, English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, ICT, and other unmentioned subjects.

  3. How do I find a tutor?

    4. You can look for tutors by subject, grade level, language, location, and teaching preference.

    6. Yes, students can select the learning method that works best for them.

  6. How can I become a tutor?
