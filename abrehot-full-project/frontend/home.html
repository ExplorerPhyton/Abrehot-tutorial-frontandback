Find the Right Tutor for Every Student!

Connect with qualified tutors for KG to Grade 12, anytime and anywhere.

Whether your child needs help with Mathematics, English, Science, or any other subject, Abrehot Online Tutorials makes it easy to find experienced tutors who match your learning needs. Choose from online or in-person sessions and book at a time that works for you. Browse tutor profiles, compare qualifications and prices, and book online or in-person tutoring sessions with ease. Our platform supports English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo, and Tigrinya, making quality education more accessible for everyone.

How It Works
Create an Account

to create a free account as a parent or a student to unlock personalized features, book a session and connect with tutors.
Search for Tutors

Find qualified tutors by searching based on subject, grade level, language, location, or teaching mode on our Tutors page .
Compare Tutors

Browse tutor profiles, compare qualifications, experience, ratings, and hourly rates to choose the best tutor.
Book a Session

Select your preferred date and time, then book an online or in-person tutoring session with your chosen tutor.

FAQs:

How do I book a tutoring session?

First create an account, then browse tutors, choose one, and finally complete the booking form.

How are tutors verified?

Each tutor is reviewed before being approved on the platform.

Can I choose online or in-person tutoring?

Yes, you can select your preferred teaching mode when booking.

Who can use this platform?

Parents and students from KG to Grade 12 can find qualified tutors.Tutors can apply to become verified tutors on this platform where they can support students and make money on the side.

Can I become a tutor?

Yes, simply sign up, complete the tutor application form and submit your details.
