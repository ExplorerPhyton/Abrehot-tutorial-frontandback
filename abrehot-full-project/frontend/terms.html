2. Cancellation & Rescheduling

To maintain operational structure, scheduling changes are governed by the following timelines:

  • Notice Over 24 Hours: Sessions may be rescheduled or canceled without penalty, subject to instructor availability.
  • Notice Between 12 and 24 Hours: One rescheduling opportunity may be granted. Repeated late requests will result in the session being marked as used.
  • Notice Under 12 Hours: Late cancellations are non-refundable and will be treated as a completed/used session.

3. Punctuality & No-Show Policy

  • Student No-Show: Tutors will wait for up to 15 minutes past the scheduled start time. If the student fails to join within 15 minutes, the session is recorded as a student no-show and deducted as a completed lesson.

FAQs:

How do I book a tutoring session?

First create an account, then browse tutors, choose one, and finally complete the booking form.

How are tutors verified?

Each tutor is reviewed before being approved on the platform.

Can I choose online or in-person tutoring?

Yes, you can select your preferred teaching mode when booking.

Who can use this platform?

Parents and students from KG to Grade 12 can find qualified tutors.Tutors can apply to become verified tutors on this platform where they can support students and make money on the side.

Can I become a tutor?

Yes, simply sign up, complete the tutor application form and submit your details.
