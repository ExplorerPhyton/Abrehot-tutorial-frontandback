FAQs:

How do I book a tutoring session?

First create an account, then browse tutors, choose one, and finally complete the booking form.

How are tutors verified?

Each tutor is reviewed before being approved on the platform.

Can I choose online or in-person tutoring?

Yes, you can select your preferred teaching mode when booking.

Who can use this platform?

Parents and students from KG to Grade 12 can find qualified tutors.Tutors can apply to become verified tutors on this platform where they can support students and make money on the side.

Can I become a tutor?

Yes, simply sign up, complete the tutor application form and submit your details.
